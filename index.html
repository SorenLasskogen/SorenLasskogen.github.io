01 — Bachelor thesis · 2026

SpellBound & the O.R.B.S.

Two custom 3D-printed controllers and a co-op game built around them. To cast a spell, both players have to twist their orb to the right symbols and press it together — neither can win alone. The thesis tested whether tangible interfaces like these make people collaborate more than ordinary gamepads.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • ESP32
  • ESP-NOW
  • 3D printing
  • User study

02 — Virtual reality · 2025

TACET VR

An immersive VR extension of TACET, a short film by director Ahmed El Kholy, made in close collaboration with him. Instead of watching the story from the outside, you step into the protagonist’s world through environmental storytelling, spatial audio and cinematic lighting.

  • Unity
  • C#
  • Meta Quest 3
  • XR Interaction Toolkit

03 — Interactive installation · 2025

Walking on Water

An interactive floor projection that follows you. A ceiling camera tracks your position with a YOLO model, a bubble of light moves under your feet, and where you stand reshapes an underwater soundscape. Clap or shout, and the water pulses.

  • Python
  • YOLO11
  • OpenCV
  • TouchDesigner
  • Ableton Live
  • OSC
View project →

04 — Interactive sound · 2025

IPASIF

A modular, wireless framework that helps artists build interactive sound installations. Sensors on ESP32 boards stream data into Pure Data for sound and TouchDesigner for visuals — so a thrown ball, a turned box or a slider can play the room.

  • ESP32
  • ESP-NOW
  • OSC
  • Pure Data
  • TouchDesigner
  • MediaPipe
